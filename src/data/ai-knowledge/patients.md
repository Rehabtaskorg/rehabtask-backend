# For Patients

This covers individual patients arranging therapy for themselves. Agencies managing multiple
patients should see the agency section.

## Setting up an account

Create an account on RehabTask and choose "customer", then "individual". You will provide your
personal details and the documents needed to verify your account, including a therapy order if
one applies.

Your account is reviewed before you can book. You will be notified when it is approved.

## How to find a therapist

There are two ways:

**Browse and request directly** — search therapists by discipline, location and availability.
Open a therapist's profile to see their experience, specialties, rates and reviews, then send
them a request. Only that therapist sees it.

**Post a request** — describe what you need and let matching therapists come to you. Therapists
whose licence and service area fit will be notified and can send offers.

## How booking works

1. You create a request with the type of therapy, location, preferred date and any details
2. Therapists send offers with their rate and proposed date
3. You review the offers and accept the one you want
4. You pay, and the money is held securely
5. The therapist visits and marks the session complete
6. You confirm it was done, and the therapist is paid

## Reviewing offers

Each offer shows the therapist's rate, the proposed date and their message. You can accept it,
decline it, or ask for a change.

Offers expire if you do not respond — by default after 48 hours.

## Payments

You pay when you accept an offer. The money is held securely and is not released to the
therapist until the work is done and you have confirmed it.

For a multi-session booking, payment is released one session at a time as each is confirmed.

## Confirming a session

After a therapist marks a session complete you will be asked to confirm it. Confirming releases
that session's payment.

If you do not confirm within 72 hours, the system confirms it automatically.

If something was not right, you can request a revision instead of confirming. The therapist is
notified and can respond.

## Your appointments

All your bookings and their sessions are in **My Bookings**. Each booking shows the therapist,
the dates, the session status and the payment summary.

## Cancellations

You can request to cancel a booking. The therapist is notified and has 24 hours to respond. If
they do not respond, the cancellation is approved automatically.

Refunds are returned to you through your connected account.

## Leaving a review

Once a booking is complete you can rate the therapist on three things — communication, timely
documentation and professionalism — and leave a comment. Reviews appear on the therapist's
profile.

## Messaging

You can message a therapist about a booking or request through the Messages section.
