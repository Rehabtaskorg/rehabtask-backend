# For Agencies

Home health agencies use RehabTask to find therapists for the patients they manage.

## How to register

Create an account and choose "customer", then "agency". You will provide your business details
and upload the documents required to verify the agency.

Your account is reviewed before you can post requests or book. You will be notified when it is
approved.

## Adding and managing patients

Agencies add the patients they manage under **My Patients**. Each patient record holds their
name, contact details, date of birth and certification period.

A patient must exist before you can create a request for them, so that the therapist knows who
the visit is for.

## How referrals work

When you create a request you assign it to one of your patients. The therapist sees the patient
details they need in order to provide care.

## Finding therapists

Two ways, the same as any customer:

**Browse and send a direct request** — search by discipline, location and availability, open a
therapist's profile, and send them a request. Only that therapist sees it.

**Post a public request** — therapists whose licence type and service area match are notified
and can send offers.

## How bookings work

1. Create a request, assigned to one of your patients
2. Therapists send offers with their rates and proposed dates
3. Accept the offer you want
4. Pay — the money is held securely
5. The therapist completes the visit and marks it done
6. Confirm it, and the therapist is paid

For multi-session bookings, payment is released session by session as each is confirmed.

## Subscription plans and visit limits

Agency accounts have a plan that sets how many visits can be booked in a billing period:

| Plan | Visits per period | Job postings |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 10 | 5 |
| Pro | 150 | Unlimited |
| Enterprise | 500 | Unlimited |
| Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |

If a booking would take you over your remaining visits, you will be told before you accept it.
Limits reset each billing period.

## Account management

Your subscription, payment method and billing history are under **Subscription** and
**Payments & Credits**.

## Confirming sessions

After a therapist marks a session complete, confirm it to release their payment. If you do not
confirm within 72 hours, the system confirms it automatically.

If something was not right, request a revision instead and the therapist can respond.

## Cancellations

You can request to cancel a booking. The therapist has 24 hours to respond, and if they do not,
the cancellation is approved automatically. Refunds return to your connected account.

## Reviews

After a booking completes you can rate the therapist on communication, timely documentation and
professionalism, and leave a comment.
