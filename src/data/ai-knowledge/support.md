# Support and Troubleshooting

## Contacting support

Email **support@rehabtask.com**.

Always direct people to support for: complaints, billing disputes, refunds, account security,
verification disputes, clinical questions, privacy concerns, anything involving a specific
patient's medical details, and anything you cannot answer.

## Logging in

**Forgot your password** — use the "Forgot password" link on the login page. A reset link is
sent to your registered email. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive.

**Two-factor authentication** — if enabled on your account, you will be asked for a code after
entering your password. It is sent by the method you chose when enabling it.

**Cannot log in at all** — contact support.

## Account approval

New therapist and customer accounts are reviewed before they can be used.

**While waiting** — you can sign in and see your application status, but cannot book or take
cases yet.

**If something is missing** — you will be told what needs correcting and can resubmit.

**If rejected** — the reason is shown on your dashboard. Contact support if you believe it was
a mistake.

Approval times are not fixed. If you have been waiting and heard nothing, contact support.

## Updating your details

Most profile details can be changed from your profile page at any time.

Some details — such as your name, date of birth or licence information — are checked during
verification. Changing them may put your account back into review so the team can confirm the
new information.

## Payment issues

**A payment failed** — check the card details and that the card allows online payments. You can
retry from the booking.

**Payment is held and not released** — this is normal. Money is held securely until the session
is complete and confirmed.

**A payout has not arrived** — payouts go to the bank account connected during onboarding, on
Stripe's standard schedule, after a session is confirmed. Contact support if it seems delayed.

**Payout setup is incomplete** — therapists must finish connecting their bank account through
Stripe before they can mark sessions complete.

## Notifications

RehabTask sends email notifications, and text messages if you opted in.

**Not receiving texts** — check that your phone number on your profile is correct and that SMS
notifications are switched on. If you previously replied STOP to a message, you will have been
unsubscribed.

**Not receiving emails** — check your spam folder and that your email address is correct.

## Common questions

**Can I use RehabTask outside the United States?** — No, RehabTask currently operates in the US
only.

**Is there a mobile app?** — Use the website on your phone; it works on mobile browsers.

**How do I delete my account?** — Contact support.
