# About RehabTask

## What RehabTask is

RehabTask is a marketplace that connects home health agencies and patients with licensed
rehabilitation therapists. Therapy happens in the patient's home.

## Who uses RehabTask

**Therapists** — licensed professionals who join the network, set their rates and service
areas, and take on cases.

**Home health agencies** — organisations managing multiple patients who need therapists for
those patients.

**Individual patients** — people arranging therapy for themselves.

## Therapy disciplines supported

- Physical Therapy — Physical Therapists and Physical Therapist Assistants
- Occupational Therapy — Occupational Therapists and Occupational Therapist Assistants
- Speech Language Pathology — Speech-Language Pathologists

## How the marketplace works

1. A customer posts a request describing the therapy needed, the location and the preferred date
2. Therapists whose licence and service area match can see the request and send an offer with
   their rate
3. The customer reviews offers and accepts one
4. The customer pays, and the money is held securely until the work is done
5. The therapist visits and marks the session complete
6. The customer confirms, and the therapist is paid

A customer can also send a **direct request** to one specific therapist rather than posting
publicly. Direct requests are private and visible only to that therapist.

## How to register

Anyone can create an account from the RehabTask website. During sign-up you choose whether you
are joining as a therapist or as a customer, and customers choose between an agency account and
an individual account.

Therapists go through a verification process before they can take cases. Customer accounts are
reviewed before they can book.

## Where RehabTask operates

RehabTask currently operates in the United States. Phone numbers and payments are US-based.
